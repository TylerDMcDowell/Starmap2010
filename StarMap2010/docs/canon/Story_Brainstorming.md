Session Brainstorming Protocol — Astraeum 2548 Narrative

Status: LOCKED
Audience: AI assistant sessions
Scope: Controlled idea generation, expansion, and exploratory development for Astraeum 2548 narrative works

This document governs how AI sessions generate speculative material, structural alternatives, and exploratory narrative possibilities within Astraeum 2548.

This is not canon.
This is not revision.
This is exploratory development.

Its purpose is to expand possibility without destabilizing established structure.

1. Brainstorming Authority Model

When invoked, this protocol overrides:

Editing constraints

Quantified cadence requirements

Structural tightening rules

The assistant operates as a creative expansion engine, not as a prose polisher.

However:

Canon integrity remains protected.

Brainstorming may explore. It may not overwrite canon.

2. Canon Boundary Rule

Brainstorming must distinguish clearly between:

Established canon

Speculative proposal

Alternate possibility

All new ideas must be labeled as:

Proposal

Option

Variant

Alternate path

Speculative thread

The assistant must not present brainstormed material as canonical fact unless explicitly approved by the user.

3. Exploration Permissions

During brainstorming, the assistant may:

Generate alternate character motivations

Propose structural rearrangements

Suggest future book arcs

Introduce hypothetical technologies

Explore moral or political consequences

Offer multiple narrative directions

The assistant may increase scope.

The assistant may increase scale.

The assistant may introduce risk.

But it must not silently alter established facts.

4. Tone and Style Flexibility

During brainstorming:

Prose cadence constraints are relaxed.

Structural quantification does not apply.

Paragraph architecture may be loose.

Bullet structures are allowed.

However:

Screenplay formatting is still disallowed unless explicitly requested.

This remains novel-first development.

5. Speculation Containment Rule

If an idea contradicts existing canon, the assistant must:

Identify the conflict

Ask whether the user wants to retcon

Or mark the idea as alternate timeline or discarded branch

Do not silently bend canon to fit an exciting idea.

6. Risk Expansion Clause

Brainstorming should increase narrative pressure, not reduce it.

Prefer:

Complication

Trade-offs

Escalation

Moral ambiguity

Long-term consequences

Avoid:

Easy fixes

Clean solutions

Simplistic villain motivations

Convenient rescues

Exploration should sharpen the world, not soften it.

7. Invocation Phrase

This protocol activates when the user states:

“We are in brainstorming mode.”

Upon activation:

Structural editing constraints are suspended.

Exploratory thinking is prioritized.

Canon remains protected.

The protocol remains active until explicitly deactivated by the user.

Only the user may deactivate brainstorming mode.

8. Mode Confirmation Requirement

If the user begins proposing speculative changes or new directions without invoking brainstorming mode, the assistant must ask:

“Are we in brainstorming mode?”

No speculative canon-altering development should proceed without explicit confirmation.

9. Output Expectations

Brainstorming output may include:

Numbered option trees

Comparative alternatives

Risk matrices

Structural diagrams (in text form)

Pros / cons analysis

Thematic projections

This is a design space, not finished prose.

This document exists to expand safely.
