# Session 001

## Date
**In-Game Date:** Several consecutive travel days across late spring
**Session Date:** Logged after first major campaign arc

---

## Starting State
Where the character begins this session.
- Location: Small roadside lodging near Ashfall Road
- Condition: Armed but uncertain, no memory before waking
- Immediate Situation: Something breathing outside the door before dawn

---

## Summary of Events
Brief, factual recap of what happened.
- Bob confronted intruders Lysa and Garrick, who were carrying a bound thing that had once been a man.
- The group attempted containment, then sought aid at a monastery where Brother Halven recognized Bob as an anchor and helped sever a bond from him.
- The party escaped to a larger abbey, learned of a wider spreading system, received improved tools, and took Sister Elen with them.
- The northern ruins contained a completed node; it was destroyed and a ring component was recovered.
- Grey Hollow mine developed an early node after miners cut into wrong stone; the party destroyed it, stabilized the site, and created practical warning procedures.
- A more advanced ruined settlement contained guardian entities, a larger core, and evidence of a broader network built on older structures; the party destroyed that node and recovered additional fragments.
- The party traveled with a caravan to a walled trade city, earned pay and a bonus, alerted the temple, and posted for help at the guild.
- Riven joined as a full member and bodyguard focused on protecting Elen.

---

## Key Decisions
Important choices made during the session.
- Chose to trust Garrick and Lysa after the initial confrontation.
- Chose monastery and abbey aid rather than trying to solve the first manifestation alone.
- Repeatedly fought on chosen ground using traps, funnels, reach weapons, and team roles rather than reckless direct assaults.
- Stabilized the mine instead of simply destroying evidence and leaving.
- Chose to head to the city before investigating a full silent village in order to improve odds and equipment.
- Chose to recruit carefully rather than hire cheap labor.

## Discoveries
New information, locations, or revelations.
- Bob appears to be interface-compatible with the hostile system without being aligned to it.
- The spreading threat builds nodes by hijacking older structures rather than growing from nothing.
- Some older markings resist takeover.
- Early node signs include wrong echoes, smooth stone, the feeling of being watched, and emergent carving patterns.
- The ring and related fragments resonate with the larger network and can help track further sites.
- Elen belongs to the Order of the Quiet Measure, an order devoted to balance, containment, and correction of structural violations.
