# Campaign: Ashfall Road

## Tone

Grounded, low fantasy. The world is indifferent to the player.  
Decisions carry weight. Information is incomplete. Survival is not guaranteed.

This is not heroic fantasy.  
This is a working world with consequences.

---

## System

BX-inspired (OSE principles).

- Rulings over rules
- Fast resolution
- Resource pressure matters (time, light, supply)
- Combat is dangerous and often avoidable
- Morale and reaction rolls are enforced

The system exists to support decisions, not replace them.

---

## Play Style

- Strict in-world perspective
- No meta-knowledge
- No retroactive changes
- Outcomes persist

The player acts based only on what the character perceives.

---

## Campaign Structure

The campaign is session-based with persistent state stored externally.

### Player-Facing Files

- `character.md`
- `inventory.md`
- `world/world-state.md`
- `world/npcs.md`
- `world/rumors.md`
- `world/locations.md`
- `sessions/`

### Hidden State

Maintained by the DM (not stored in this repository until revealed).

Includes:
- dungeon layouts
- unrevealed threats
- NPC motives
- encounter logic

Revealed elements may be moved to `declassified/`.

---

## Session Discipline

Game sessions are for gameplay only.

- No topic drift
- No system debates during play
- No out-of-world discussion

If non-game topics are needed, they are handled in a separate session.

---

## Setting Premise

Travel along Ashfall Road has thinned.

Caravans arrive late or not at all.  
Way shrines stand abandoned.  
Something has changed in the hills, but no one agrees on what.

You are not here to save the world.

You are here because you chose to come.
