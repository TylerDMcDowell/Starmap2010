# Campaign: Ashfall Road

## Tone
Grounded, low fantasy. The world is indifferent to the player. Decisions carry weight. Information is incomplete. Survival is not guaranteed. This is not heroic fantasy. This is a working world with consequences.

---

## System
BX-inspired (OSE principles).

- Rulings over rules
- Fast resolution
- Resource pressure matters (time, light, supply)
- Combat is dangerous and often avoidable
- Morale and reaction rolls are enforced

The system exists to support decisions, not replace them.

---

## Play Style
- Strict in-world perspective during play
- No meta-knowledge in active sessions
- No retroactive changes
- Outcomes persist

The player acts based only on what the character perceives.

---

## Campaign Structure
The campaign is session-based with persistent state stored externally.

### Player-Facing Files
- `character.md`
- `inventory.md`
- `world/world-state.md`
- `world/npcs.md`
- `world/rumors.md`
- `world/locations.md`
- `sessions/`

### Hidden State Maintained by the DM
Not stored in this repository until revealed.

Includes:
- unrevealed site layouts
- unrevealed threats
- hidden motives
- encounter logic

Revealed elements may be moved to `declassified/` later if desired.

---

## Session Discipline
Game sessions are for gameplay only.

- No topic drift
- No system debates during play
- No out-of-world discussion during active play

If non-game topics are needed, they are handled in a separate session.

---

## Setting Premise
Travel along Ashfall Road has thinned. Caravans arrive late or not at all. Way shrines stand abandoned. Something is building itself through old places, quiet roads, ruined foundations, and cut stone. Most people only see the signs too late.

You are not wandering blindly anymore.

You and your companions are now tracking a spreading network of hostile nodes that attach to older structures and use people to grow.
