# Locations

## Known Settlements

### Name: Grey Hollow
- Type: Trade town
- Region: Along Ashfall Road
- Status: Active, cautious
- Key Features: Inn, smithy, guild board, small local temple, mine outside town
- Notes: Mine was temporarily stabilized after node growth was discovered and stopped there.

### Name: Unnamed Trade City
- Type: Walled trade city
- Region: Southeast of Grey Hollow on the main trade route
- Status: Active, crowded, well supplied
- Key Features: Temple, guild hall, merchants, inns, smith district, stables
- Notes: Current base of operations at session end.

## Known Sites
Places the player knows exist, whether visited or not.

### Name: Northern Ruins
- Type: Old ruin complex
- Region: North of the monastery
- Known Information: Contained a completed node and ancient underlying structure
- Status: Explored
- Notes: First major node destroyed there; ring component recovered.

### Name: River Road Monastery
- Type: Monastic watch house
- Region: Near the river road west of the first roadside lodging
- Known Information: Brother Halven and his order helped sever the first bond from Bob
- Status: Explored
- Notes: First site where Bob’s anchor nature was recognized.

### Name: Abbey of Prior Kessan
- Type: Larger religious stronghold
- Region: West of the road network reached after the monastery
- Known Information: Holds knowledge about the spreading system and supplied the party with improved tools
- Status: Explored
- Notes: First place to formally recognize nodes as part of a larger structure.

### Name: Grey Hollow Mine
- Type: Working mine
- Region: Outside Grey Hollow
- Known Information: Early-stage node growth began when miners cut into the wrong stone
- Status: Explored
- Notes: Cleared and stabilized with warning procedures, clerical oversight, and work limits.

### Name: Ruined Settlement (advanced node site)
- Type: Abandoned settlement
- Region: Southeast beyond Grey Hollow
- Known Information: Contained a more advanced node with guardian manifestations and an older foundation beneath it
- Status: Explored
- Notes: Major node destroyed; multiple fragments recovered.

### Name: Darrow’s Rest
- Type: Waypoint village
- Region: Off-route beyond the city
- Known Information: Avoided by caravans; reported as quiet and wrong
- Status: Unvisited
- Notes: Strong lead for future investigation.

## Explored Locations

### Name: Grey Hollow Mine
- Type: Mine
- Region: Grey Hollow outskirts
- Summary: Active dig site where workers began hearing wrong sounds and carving patterns started to appear below ground.
- Notable Findings: Early node, worker conversion, patterned growth on stone, practical warning signs for future work.
- Known Exits / Approaches: Main mine entrance, known interior fork, lower worked chamber.
- Remaining Questions: What exactly the miners cut into, and how many similar weak points exist.
- Notes: Site was physically damaged and warded to slow reactivation.

### Name: Ruined Settlement
- Type: Settlement / node site
- Region: Southeast wilderness
- Summary: Larger-scale node construction site using multiple converted workers and guardian entities.
- Notable Findings: Older resistant structural layer beneath the invading pattern, fragment cluster, evidence of organized expansion.
- Known Exits / Approaches: Open approach from broken ridge line; center structure formerly exposed.
- Remaining Questions: Where the larger network originates and how many active sites remain.
- Notes: Site broken, salted, and partially disrupted; not guaranteed permanently dead.

## Travel Routes

### Route: Grey Hollow to Trade City
- Connects: Grey Hollow ↔ Walled trade city
- Estimated Travel Time: About 2 days with caravan pace
- Known Hazards: Quiet zones near old structures, possible spread sites off-road
- Status: Open
- Notes: Party earned caravan pay and bonus while traveling this route.

### Route: Trade City to Darrow’s Rest
- Connects: Walled trade city ↔ Darrow’s Rest
- Estimated Travel Time: 1–2 days off the main road, depending on route
- Known Hazards: Reported silence, abandoned traffic, likely node activity
- Status: Avoided by most travelers
- Notes: Strong future lead.
