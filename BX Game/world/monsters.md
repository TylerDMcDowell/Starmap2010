# Monsters

## Evolution Phases

1. Basic corruption
2. Adhesion entities (binding)
3. Coordinators (tactical)
4. Unified field (area-scale)
5. Transition state (adaptive system)

## Behavior
- Learns during combat
- Coordinates attacks
- Uses environment

## Weakness
- Oil prevents cohesion
- Water disrupts
- Distillation destroys anchors

## Notes
- Prepared sites amplify power
